== Inputs

Review these inputs:

* the status of the actions from the previous management review
* the changes in the context, the scope, and the requirements of the interested parties (see the compliance register)
* the assessments of the internal controls of the home, and the annual wheel
* the incidents and the near misses of the year (see the incident register)
* the results of the risk assessment, and the status of the risk treatment
* the security targets of the year, and how well they were met
* the opportunities for improvement
* if NIS2 applies: the status of the cybersecurity risk-management measures of NIS2 Article 21 (see the NIS2 requirements in the compliance register)
