== Purpose

This policy states the commitment of the management to information security, and the high-level objectives that everyone in the organisation follows. The policy covers the scope of the ISMS, which is defined in the business context analysis. It supports the business objectives of the organisation.

== High-level objectives

* The organisation protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of customer information and of its own information.
* Information is classified and protected according to its classification, for example with access control and encryption. Access is given only when the work needs it. The cryptography rules give the details of encryption.
* Information security management is risk based. Risks are assessed and treated according to the risk management process. Security-aware people, documented processes, and suitable security technologies manage the risks.
* Incidents and weaknesses are reported without undue delay, according to the incident management process.
* The organisation uses AI systems in a controlled way. Information is given to an AI service only when the service is approved for that classification. People check the results of AI before they use them. The AI policy gives the details.

== Commitments

The management commits to:

* meet the information security requirements that apply to the organisation: laws, regulations, contracts, and the requirements of customers and other interested parties
* improve the information security management system continually.
