== Registrations

Some regulations require that the organisation registers with an authority before anything happens. For example, an entity under NIS2 gives its information to the competent authority (NIS2 Directive, Article 3(4)). The national law sets the authority and the deadline, for example section 41 of the Cybersecurity Act in Finland.
