== Scope

This review covers the asset register of the home: the primary assets, the supporting assets, the suppliers, and the development projects.

== Review

* Check that the new business processes, services, information systems, devices, and suppliers since the last review are in the register.
* Check that each asset has an owner and an up-to-date classification.
* Check that each AI tool in use is an AI asset, and that its assessment is up to date.
* Check that the removed assets have been archived, and that their information has been returned or disposed of.
* Check that the assets that need a risk evaluation have risks in the risk register.
