== Security roles

The central security roles of the organisation. Set the assignee and the deputy of each role. The roles of a single development project are assigned in that project.

== Review of the role assignments

* Check that each role has an assignee and a deputy.
* Check that each assignee and deputy has confirmed the role.
* Check that the management has approved the assignments. Record who approved them, and when.
* Check that the assignees have the competence that their roles need.
* Check the segregation of duties: nobody approves or reviews their own work. For example, a document owner does not review their own document, and an administrator does not review their own access rights. Where the duties cannot be separated, record a compensating measure, for example a review by the management.
