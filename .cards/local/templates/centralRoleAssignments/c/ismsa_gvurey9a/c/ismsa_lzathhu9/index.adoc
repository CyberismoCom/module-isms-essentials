Follows the changes in regulation, and keeps the compliance register, the authority contacts, and the records retention plan up to date.

*Authority:* Can ask the owners of controls and records for the information that compliance needs, and set the deadlines for it.

*Competence:* The laws and the standards that apply to the organisation.

Set the person in the role in the *Assignee* field, and the deputy in the *Deputy* field. The deputy does the work of the role when the assignee is away. The 24-hour deadlines of NIS2 and the CRA apply on every day of the year.
