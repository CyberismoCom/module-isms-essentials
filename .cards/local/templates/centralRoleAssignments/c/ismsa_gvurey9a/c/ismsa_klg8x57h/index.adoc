Gives the public statements about incidents, communicates with customers, and reports incidents to authorities when the incident management process requires it.

*Authority:* Can make statements about incidents for the organisation, and send the notifications to authorities.

*Competence:* Crisis communication, and the reporting duties of NIS2, the CRA, and the GDPR.

Set the person in the role in the *Assignee* field, and the deputy in the *Deputy* field. The deputy does the work of the role when the assignee is away. The 24-hour deadlines of NIS2 and the CRA apply on every day of the year.
