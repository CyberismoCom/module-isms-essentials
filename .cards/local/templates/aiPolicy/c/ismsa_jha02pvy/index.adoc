== Purpose

This policy states how the organisation uses artificial intelligence (AI) in a safe, legal, and responsible way. It supports the information security policy, and it applies to all employees and contractors who use AI in their work.

== Scope

This policy covers the AI tools and services that the organisation uses, and the AI features in other services. AI in the products of the organisation is covered in the secure development process.

== High-level objectives

* Only approved AI tools are used for work. The AI assets in the asset register are the approved tools.
* Information is given to an AI tool only when the tool is approved for that classification of information.
* Customer information is given to an AI tool only when the contract with the customer allows it.
* The data of the organisation is not used to train AI models, unless the owner of the data approves it.
* People check the results of AI before they use them. The person who uses AI is responsible for the result.
* AI agents and integrations get only the access that the task needs. A person approves the actions that cannot be undone, for example sending, deleting, or publishing.
* The organisation tells customers and other interested parties when AI has a significant role in its work or its content, and when the law or a contract requires it.
* The organisation does not use AI for prohibited practices of the EU AI Act. High-risk use of AI needs the approval of the management before it starts.

== Commitments

The management commits to:

* meet the requirements that apply to the use of AI: laws, regulations, contracts, and the requirements of customers and other interested parties
* improve the management of AI continually.

== Competence

Each employee and contractor who uses AI in their work gets the training that they need to use it safely (AI literacy). The training covers at least the approved tools, the information allowed in each tool, the limits of AI results, and how to report problems.

== New AI tools

A new AI tool is approved before it is used for work. The owner of the tool adds it to the asset register as an AI asset, and assesses it. The assessment covers at least the allowed information, the use of training data, the EU AI Act risk category, and the need for human oversight.

== Incidents

Problems with AI are reported without undue delay, according to the incident management process. Examples are information given to a wrong tool, harmful or wrong results that were used, and unexpected actions of an AI agent.
