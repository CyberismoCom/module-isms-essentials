== Checklist

The checklist follows the basic hygiene practices that Traficom recommends for NIS2 entities (11.1 to 11.13). For each practice, record if it is in place, the evidence, and the gaps.

[cols="3,5,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Practice | What to check | In place | Evidence or gaps

| Multi-factor authentication (NIS2 21(2)(j))
| Multi-factor authentication is on for remote access, for administrators, and for the services on the internet.

| Administrator rights are in separate accounts, only the people who need them have them, and they are not used for email or browsing.

| Systems and devices get updates regularly. Critical updates are installed within days, as the process for managing vulnerabilities and updates says.

| Services and devices are configured securely. Default passwords are changed, and unused services are off.
