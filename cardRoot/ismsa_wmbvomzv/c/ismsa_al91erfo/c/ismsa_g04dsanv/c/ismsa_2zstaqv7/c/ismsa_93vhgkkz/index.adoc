== Scope

This review covers the risk register of the home, and the risk treatment.

== Review

* Check that the risks of new and changed assets have been identified and analysed.
* Check that each risk has an owner, a treatment decision, and a residual risk.
* Check that the risk treatment plans are on schedule.
* Check that the accepted risks are at or below the acceptable risk level, or that the management has accepted them.
* Check that the threats and the incidents since the last review are reflected in the risks.
